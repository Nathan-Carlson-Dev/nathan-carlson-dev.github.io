Notes and Projects:

Aperiodic mechanical stirring of a material in a 2D fluid

In Phillip Boyland's work on mechanical stirring, he studied the braiding of the trajectories of stirrers and connected it to the theory of mapping class groups. This resulted in a statement about time-periodic stirring and the mixing of the vorticity (or more generally, a passively transported scalar material). This project tries to explore how loosening the constraint of time-periodicity affects the mixing properties. Deforming the stirring so that the trajectories become time-periodic again and studying how it deforms allow us to determine when a similar mixing result can be reasonably obtained.
