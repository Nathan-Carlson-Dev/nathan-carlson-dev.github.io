Interests:

I find the topological classification of structures very fascinating! My first exposure being knots and braids. Now, I have been very interested in the applications of these types of classifications in physics, particularly, fluids. The following is a brief list of topics that I have been interested in:

  • Classification of coadjoint orbits of 2D area-preserving diffeomorphisms
  • Topological effects of mechanical Stirring of 2D Fluids
  • Braiding of point vortex systems
  • The relationship between quantum systems, fluids, and geometry (e.g. geometric quantization, Madelung transform)
  • Euler-Arnold type equations and their behavior
